OAZIS akkauntini o'chirish

Ushbu sahifa Oazis Company tomonidan ishlab chiqilgan OAZIS ilovasidagi akkauntni va unga bog'liq ma'lumotlarni o'chirish tartibini tushuntiradi.

1-usul: ilova ichida (tavsiya etiladi)

  1. OAZIS ilovasini oching va akkauntingizga kiring.
  2. Profil bo'limiga o'ting va Sozlamalarni oching.
  3. Akkauntni o'chirish tugmasini bosing va tasdiqlang.

Akkaunt darhol o'chiriladi va siz tizimdan chiqasiz.

2-usul: ilovasiz so'rov yuborish

Ilovaga kira olmasangiz, +998 71 200 53 53 raqamiga qo'ng'iroq qiling va akkauntga bog'langan elektron pochta yoki telefon raqamini ayting. Egalikni tasdiqlash uchun shu manzilga kod yuboriladi. So'rov 7 ish kuni ichida bajariladi.

Nimalar o'chiriladi

  • ism, familiya, elektron pochta, telefon, manzil, parol va Google identifikatori;
  • profil rasmi;
  • kurslarga yozilish, darslar progressi, test va mashq natijalari, ballar, yutuqlar, sertifikatlar;
  • chat xabarlari va yuborilgan fayllar, bildirishnomalar;
  • push-bildirishnoma tokeni.

Nimalar saqlanib qoladi

Buxgalteriya va soliq qonunchiligi talab qiladigan xarid yozuvlari (mahsulot, summa, sana, to'lov holati) shaxsingizga bog'lanmagan holda qonunda belgilangan muddat davomida saqlanadi. Zaxira nusxalardagi ma'lumotlar 30 kun ichida o'chadi.

Diqqat: o'chirilgan akkauntni tiklab bo'lmaydi; sotib olingan kurslarga kirish ham yo'qoladi.

Delete your OAZIS account

How to delete your account and associated data in the OAZIS app by Oazis Company.

In the app

  1. Open OAZIS and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Call +998 71 200 53 53 with the email or phone number linked to your account. We verify ownership with a code and complete the request within 7 business days.

Data deleted

Name, email, phone, address, password, Google ID, profile photo, enrollments, progress, results, points, achievements, certificates, chat messages and files, notifications and push token.

Data retained

Purchase records required by accounting and tax law (product, amount, date, status) are kept without being linked to you for the legally required period. Backups are purged within 30 days.
